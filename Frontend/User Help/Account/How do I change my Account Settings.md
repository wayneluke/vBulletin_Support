You can adjust your account settings by selecting your username in the top right corner of the vBulletin site, which opens a dropdown menu where you choose “User Settings.”

On the settings page, you may see up to five tabs: Profile, Settings, Privacy, Notifications, and Subscriptions. These include:

- Profile – This shows public information visible to everyone; you can limit access through the [++Privacy++](NSURL0x7641aa80c0) tab.
- Account – This tab holds account settings and lets you shape how the system presents content.
- Privacy – This controls who can view your information.
- Notifications – This manages alerts for content you follow.
- Subscriptions – If available, you can buy or cancel subscriptions here; they may grant extra permissions, improved posting rights, or simply support the site.

Additional tabs may appear based on your permissions and site offerings.
